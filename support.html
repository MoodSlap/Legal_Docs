MoodSlap Support & Guide

Welcome to the MoodSlap command center. If you're lost, confused, or just looking for an excuse to procrastinate... you came to the right place. Our "support staff" will take it from here.

App Guide & FAQ

How do I actually use this app? What's the point?
The Ego
Look, it's simple. We are your new command center. You can track your tasks, write detailed notes, sync your Apple iCal or any Webcal, check the weather, and even create shared shopping lists for your groceries. You pick one of us to hold you accountable for all of it. Whenever you want a nice, validating pat on the back, just tap the "Mood" button on the main screen. And whenever you are procrastinating or just need a brutal reality check, tap "Slap". We track your streaks. Don't disappoint us.
Why are there different characters? Who do I pick?
Lori Cute
Because one vibe doesn't fit all, bestie! Need someone to hype you up like a loyal best friend? That's me. Need military discipline? Pick the Colonel. Need to feel corporate pressure? Pick The Boss. You can switch us out anytime on the main screen based on exactly what kind of emotional damage you need today.
How does the Task Tracker work? Will it yell at me?
Fitness Coach
You bet it will! You add tasks. You set deadlines. If you enable push notifications, we WILL remind you to get off the couch. No excuses. Mark them complete to build a streak, or ignore them and watch your dossier fill up with failures. We track your completion rate, so don't even try to hide.
How do I add tasks and connect my calendar?
Future Hubby
Darling, we want our future to be strictly organized, don't we? You can easily write tasks right in the Task Tracker. Even better, you can connect your Apple iCal or any Webcal directly so everything synced up perfectly. Just go to settings and toggle "Sync with Calendar". I'll be here waiting while you plan our day!
Can I write down my thoughts or notes?
Psychologist
Journaling is excellent for cognitive processing. Yes, within any task, you can open and write detailed notes. Jot down sub-tasks, vent your frustrations, or keep track of your brilliant ideas. We log it all for your mental well-being and productivity analysis.
Can I check the weather in the app?
Karma
Everything is connected, including the weather and your mood. On the main screen, simply tap the character once to see today's weather. Tap twice (tap-tap) to see tomorrow's forecast, and tap three times (tap-tap-tap) to see the week's outlook. The universe provides, and so does MoodSlap.
I see a Shopping List feature. How does it work?
Sugar Boy
Oh babe, the shopping list is absolutely fabulous! You can type in as many products as your heart desires. And the best part? You can share the list with anyone. So when I need my matcha latte ingredients, I just send the list right over. Simple and stylish!
Can I change the screen to dark mode?
The Boss
I prefer working in the dark, it increases my focus. Yes, you can switch the entire application between a clean White screen and a sleek Dark screen. Go to the Settings tab, find "Appearance", and change it. Time is money, stop staring at the bright screen if it hurts your eyes. Let's get back to work.
What exactly do the "Mood" and "Slap" buttons do?
Crazy Wifey
Ugh, isn't it obvious?! If you did good or like to be praised, you tap "Mood" for support, sweet praise, and motivation. If you PROCRASTINATED or failed, you tap "Slap" for some tough love and a reality check! Don't come crying to me if you press Slap when you didn't earn your praise!
Are there Home Screen Widgets, and can I change their colors?
Lori Cute
Omg, yesss bestie! We have a whole bunch of cute widgets to decorate your home screen! We have small ones, big ones, task lists, and character moods. And here's the best part: you can change all the aesthetic colors to match your vibe. Go customize them right now, it's so fetch!
I'm slacking off. Will this actually make me productive?

LISTEN UP, RECRUIT! IF YOU LACK DISCIPLINE, WE WILL INSTILL IT. THIS ISN'T JUST A TOY; IT IS A TACTICAL OPERATION FOR YOUR LIFE. YOU LOG YOUR TASKS, YOU EXECUTE THEM, AND YOU REPORT BACK. IF YOU FAIL, THE DOSSIER WILL RECORD YOUR INCOMPETENCE. DISMISSED!
What is the "Dossier" and why is it analyzing me?
Psychologist
The Dossier is a reflection of your behavioral patterns. We track your task completion rates, which moods you lean towards, and generate a Weekly Roast to summarize your progress - or lack thereof. It's not random. We are analyzing your productivity habits. It's for your own good, really.
I bought a new phone or reinstalled the app. Where are my characters?
Awer Thiks
Hey there... don't worry about it. Just go to the Settings screen (the gear icon) and tap "Restore Purchases". As long as you are logged in with the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase, your subscriptions and premium characters will reactivate immediately. Now let's just chill...
How do I cancel my subscription?
Crazy Wifey
Oh, so you're just giving up on us? Typical. Fine. All subscriptions are handled by Apple natively. Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name at the very top, tap "Subscriptions", find MoodSlap, and tap Cancel. I'm fine with it. I'm FINE.
