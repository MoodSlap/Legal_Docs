Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I restore my purchases?
If you've reinstalled the app or switched to a new device, you can restore your premium subscriptions by going to the Settings screen (gear icon) and tapping "Restore Purchases". Ensure you are logged in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.
How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
All subscriptions are managed securely by Apple. To cancel or change your subscription, open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name at the top, tap "Subscriptions", and select MoodSlap to manage your plan.
Why isn't the Home Screen widget updating?
Home Screen widgets are updated periodically by iOS. To ensure it updates frequently, make sure "Background App Refresh" is enabled for MoodSlap in your iPhone's Settings. Also, check that your device isn't in "Low Power Mode", which restricts background updates.
How does Family & Friends sharing work?
You can share tasks and lists with others by linking accounts. Generate a sharing code in the App Settings and securely send it to your friend/family member. Once they enter the code in their app, your accounts will be linked for shared productivity... and roasted consequences.
