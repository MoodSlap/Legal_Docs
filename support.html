MoodSlap Support & Guide

Welcome to the MoodSlap command center. If you're lost, confused, or just looking for an excuse to procrastinate... you came to the right place. Our "support staff" will take it from here.

App Guide & FAQ

How do I actually use this app? What's the point?
[The Ego 🌌]: Look, it's simple. You create tasks you need to do. Then, you pick one of us to hold you accountable. When you finish a task, tap the "Mood" button for a nice, validating pat on the back. If you're procrastinating and being lazy, tap "Slap" for a brutal reality check. We track your streaks. Don't disappoint us.
Why are there different characters? Who do I pick?
[Lori Cute 💅]: Because one vibe doesn't fit all, bestie! Need someone to hype you up like a loyal best friend? That's me. Need military discipline? Pick the Colonel. Need to feel corporate pressure? Pick The Boss. You can switch us out anytime on the main screen based on exactly what kind of emotional damage you need today.
How does the Task Tracker work? Will it yell at me?
[Fitness Coach 🏋️‍♂️]: You bet it will! You add tasks. You set deadlines. If you enable push notifications, we WILL remind you to get off the couch. No excuses. Mark them complete to build a streak, or ignore them and watch your dossier fill up with failures. We track your completion rate, so don't even try to hide.
What is the "Dossier" and why is it analyzing me?
[Psychologist 🧐]: The Dossier is a reflection of your behavioral patterns. We track your task completion rates, which moods you lean towards, and generate a Weekly Roast to summarize your progress—or lack thereof. It's not random. We are analyzing your productivity habits. It's for your own good, really.
I bought a new phone or reinstalled the app. Where are my characters?
[The Boss 👔]: Panic is inefficient. Just go to the Settings screen (the gear icon) and tap "Restore Purchases". As long as you are logged in with the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase, your subscriptions and premium characters will reactivate immediately. Let's get back to work.
How do I cancel my subscription?
[Crazy Wifey 💃]: Oh, so you're just giving up on us? Typical. Fine. All subscriptions are handled by Apple natively. Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name at the very top, tap "Subscriptions", find MoodSlap, and tap Cancel. I'm fine with it. I'm FINE.
My home screen widget is stuck or isn't updating!
[The Ego 🌌]: It's not our fault, it's iOS trying to save battery. To fix it, ensure "Background App Refresh" is turned ON for MoodSlap in your iPhone's Settings. Also, if your phone is in "Low Power Mode", Apple stops updating widgets. Turn that off, open the app once, and the widget will refresh.
